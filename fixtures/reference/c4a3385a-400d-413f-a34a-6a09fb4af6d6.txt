Fred was unusually curious and observant.

Other penguins went hunting for creatures in the
sea—quite necessary, since there was no other food
in Antarctica. Fred fished less and studied the
iceberg and the sea more.

Other penguins spent much of their time with
friends and relatives. Fred was a good husband
and father, but he socialized less than average.
He frequently went off by himself to take notes
on what he had observed.

You might think that Fred was an odd bird,
perhaps the sort of penguin that others did not
want to spend time with. But that wasn't really
true. Fred was just doing what seemed right to
him. As a result, he was becoming increasingly
alarmed by what he saw.

Fred had a briefcase stuffed full of observations,
ideas, and conclusions. (Yes, a briefcase. This is a
fable.) The information was increasingly disturbing.
The information was beginning to cry out:
