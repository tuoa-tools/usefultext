Where they lived, a waste of energy kills. Everyone
in the colony knew they needed to huddle together
to survive. So they had learned to depend on each
other. They often behaved like a big family (which,
of course, can be both good and bad).

The birds were truly beautiful. Called Emperor
Penguins, they were the largest of seventeen
types of Antarctic animals that seem perpetually
to wear tuxedos.
